Is AI worth it for your business?

What your team's AI tools cost – and what they save – inside Odoo.

  • Import usage from Microsoft 365 Copilot, GitHub Copilot, ChatGPT Team or a shared sheet (CSV).
  • Hours saved from transparent benchmarks per tool, corrected by a one-question anonymous staff survey.
  • Value vs. licence costs per month, with net benefit and unused seats.
  • Per team, never per person: users are matched to employees only to find their department, nothing is stored per person, and teams under 5 people are combined.
  • Two roles: viewers see hours per team, managers see costs, licences and import.

Departments are combined when they have fewer than 5 employees. A single tool inside a larger department can still show a small group, so share the pivot with care.

Open source. Standalone dashboard, demo and MCP server: github.com/samaistudio/IsAIWorthIt
